# Labour Force Survey Italy 2013 (sample)

A sample of 1,000 observations from the Italian Labour Force Survey 2013
(Eurostat), provided for illustrative purposes.

## Usage

``` r
lfs_it_2013
```

## Format

A tibble with 1,000 rows and 6 variables:

- REFYEAR:

  Reference year

- SEX:

  Sex

- AGE:

  Age class

- STAPRO:

  Professional status

- HWACTUAL:

  Hours actually worked

- COEFF:

  Weighting factor

## Source

Eurostat Labour Force Survey
